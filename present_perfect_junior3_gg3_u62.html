📖 Grammar Reference

✅ Affirmative have / has + past participle
I have made a cake.
She has cooked dinner.
They have tried sushi.
I've / She's / They've
❌ Negative haven't / hasn't + p.p.
I haven't eaten yet.
He hasn't bought it.
We haven't tried it.
haven't = have not
hasn't = has not
❓ Questions Have / Has + subject + p.p.
Have you ever baked?
Has she finished yet?
Yes, I have. / No, I haven't.
ever = alguna vez
never = nunca · yet = todavía
